# Shemsh Project Instructions

This project is about redesigning Shemsh, a gold investment and physical bullion product.

Before doing any work in this repository, read these files:

1. `shemsh-project-context.md`
2. `shemsh-redesign-brief.md`
3. `shemsh-redesign-brief-inputs.md` only when deeper research detail is needed

## Shared Goal

Help redesign Shemsh into a high-quality financial-gold product that feels at the level of strong competitors or better.

The product must feel trustworthy, everyday, and slightly premium. It should serve:

- small retail gold investors
- large investors
- buyers of physical minted bullion

## Core Product Direction

The main differentiator is not only online gold buying. The strongest differentiator is that Shemsh mints bullion itself.

Future work should make this clear across product strategy, UX, UI, landing pages, trust content, and physical delivery flows.

## Important Decisions

- Assume all major product capabilities should be designed as active in the target experience.
- Key capabilities include buy, sell, deposit, withdraw, gold transfer, physical gold delivery, gift card, installment purchase, transaction history, invite friends, support, and profile.
- Brand tone: trustworthy, everyday, slightly premium.
- Trust should be built around physical bullion minting, Bank Kargoshaei, licenses, clear transaction receipts, transparent pricing, and support.
- Do not treat unclear business details as final facts. Mark them as open decisions or assumptions.

## Current Open Decisions

- One official source of truth for fees, limits, minimums, and maximums is still needed.
- Transfer fee messaging is inconsistent in the current product and must be resolved.
- Installment purchase conditions are not final yet.
- Physical delivery details are not final yet. Current assumption: users receive physical gold from Shemsh gold shops, and VIP delivery may be possible.
- Gift card is intended to have no business limitation unless later changed by the owner or legal constraints.

## Working Style

- Keep outputs aligned with `shemsh-redesign-brief.md`.
- If a new business decision is made, update `shemsh-project-context.md`.
- Preserve existing research and source files.
- Prefer clear Persian writing for strategy, UX, and product documents unless the user asks otherwise.
- Be honest about uncertainty. Separate observed facts, business decisions, assumptions, and open questions.
